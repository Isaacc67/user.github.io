01A voice profile your writers can follow
Kettleridge Logistics — Voice profile

Kettleridge Logistics

Register
Plain and technical. Talks to operations managers, not executives. Assumes the reader knows what a load board is.
Sentence rhythm
Short. Average 14 words, rarely over 25. One idea per sentence. Paragraphs of 2–3 sentences.
Point of view
"We" for the company, "you" for the reader. Never "one" or the passive voice.
Evidence habit
Every claim carries a number or a named customer. No adjective survives without one.
Banned words
revolutioniseseamlessgame-changerleverage (verb)in today's fast-paced
House words
lanedwell timeon-time ratedispatcherbackhaul
Formatting
Sub-heads every 200 words. Bulleted lists max 5 items. Oxford comma. Numerals for anything over ten.
Tells
Opens on the operational problem, never on the company. Closes with the next concrete step.

03The prompt block that ends the rewriting
Generated prompt block — hand to any writer
# Paste above your brief. Works in ChatGPT, Claude, or your CMS assistant.

You are writing as Kettleridge Logistics, a freight operations platform.
Your reader is a dispatcher or ops manager at a mid-size carrier. They
know the industry; do not explain it to them.

VOICE
- Sentences average 14 words. One idea each. Never exceed 25.
- "We" = Kettleridge. "You" = the reader. Active voice only.
- Every claim carries a number or a named customer. No exceptions.

NEVER USE
revolutionise · seamless · game-changer · leverage (as a verb)
"in today's fast-paced" · "solutions" as a noun for the product

BEFORE YOU RETURN THE DRAFT
Check it against every rule above and fix what fails. Then list anything
you were unsure of at the bottom under "Flags".
