## Description

**Stop rewriting your freelancers' work to fix the tone.**

A new client hands over brand PDFs, a two-hour kickoff recording, and three years of
old posts. Someone reads all of it and writes half a page of guidance. Every freelancer
reads that guidance differently. Drafts come back in four different voices, and you
rewrite them — not for substance, for tone. Every week, on every client.

The Client Voice Vault fixes the structure, not the talent.

Drop in the transcript, the brand docs, and five posts the client approved. Work
through a guided eleven-prompt sequence. Out comes a structured voice profile —
sentence rhythm, point of view, banned words, house words, formatting rules,
structural tells — and a prompt block your writers paste into ChatGPT, Claude, or
whatever they already use.

**What's in the file**

- **The vault database** — one row per client, with audit dates so profiles don't rot
- **The intake sequence** — 11 prompts in order, each filling a named profile field
- **The profile template** — 14 fields, specific enough to check a draft against
- **The prompt-block generator** — 4 variants: long-form, social, email, landing copy
- **The drift audit** — quarterly scoring of published work against the profile
- **The writer handoff view** — shareable with a freelancer without exposing your workspace

**Buy it if** you run content for 2+ clients through freelancers, you edit for tone more
than substance, and onboarding a writer costs you weeks.

**Skip it if** you write everything yourself for one brand, you want software that writes
the posts for you, or you need it in Airtable (Notion only at launch).
