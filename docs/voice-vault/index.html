Kettleridge Logistics — Voice profile

Kettleridge Logistics

Register
Plain and technical. Talks to operations managers, not executives. Assumes the reader knows what a load board is.
Sentence rhythm
Short. Average 14 words, rarely over 25. One idea per sentence. Paragraphs of 2–3 sentences.
Point of view
"We" for the company, "you" for the reader. Never "one" or the passive voice.
Evidence habit
Every claim carries a number or a named customer. No adjective survives without one.
Banned words
revolutioniseseamlessgame-changerleverage (verb)in today's fast-pacedsolutions
House words
lanedwell timeon-time ratedispatcherbackhaul
Formatting
Sub-heads every 200 words. Bulleted lists max 5 items. Oxford comma. Numerals for anything over ten.
Tells
Opens on the operational problem, never on the company. Closes with the next concrete step, never a summary.

Questions

Before you buy

Why not just tell ChatGPT to "write in our client's voice"?

Because "our client's voice" isn't information — it's a gesture at information. The model fills the gap with its own defaults, which is exactly why AI drafts all sound the same. A profile that names the sentence length, the banned words, and the structural tells gives it something to actually follow, and gives you something to check the draft against.

Does this work with Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini?

All of them. The output is plain text you paste in. Nothing is locked to a provider, and nothing breaks when you switch.

How long does one client take to set up?

Around 30 minutes with a kickoff transcript and five pieces of content the client has approved. The eleven-prompt sequence does the extraction; you're editing its answers, not writing from scratch.

Is this just a prompt pack?

No. Prompt packs give you prompts and leave you to manage the output. This is a database of client profiles, a pipeline that produces them, and a generated artefact your writers use. The prompts are one part of it.

I use Airtable, not Notion.
